Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026

한국어: https://kwanghyeon-cpu.github.io/gyeongjeonham/privacy.html

The developer of the Sutra Box (경전함) app and its web version (the "app") does not collect or store users' personal information.

The banner ads in the Android app are provided by Google AdMob, and Google processes the information described under "Ads" below to serve them.

If this English version and the Korean version differ, the Korean version prevails.

1. Information we collect

The developer does not collect or receive any personal information such as your name, email, phone number or location.

The app has no sign-up, no analytics tools and no developer-run servers. The web version has no ads.

2. Information stored only on your device

To make the app easier to use, the following is stored only on your device (in the browser for the web version). It never leaves your device, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear your browser data.

  • Settings such as text size, view mode (Read, Chanting, Study, Recall) and playback speed
  • Where you last stopped reading each sutra
  • Bookmarked verses and notes you write
  • Chanting playlists you make
  • Your practice record (days you read sutras, bead counts, meditation time) and dedications you write
  • Names (Dharma names) and dates of passing you enter in the 49-day rite schedule
  • Whether you turned on Today's verse, holy-day and 49-day rite notifications, and their times

Notes, dedications and the 49-day rite schedule may contain names you type yourself, but these are not sent off the device either.

When you tap Backup, the information above is saved as a file in a location you choose. The app does not send this file anywhere; you decide where to keep and share it.

3. Ads (Android app)

Google AdMob banner ads appear at the bottom of the Home and More screens of the Android app. There are no ads on the reading, listening or practice screens.

  • To show ads, measure their performance and prevent abuse, Google may collect and process your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information (device model, operating system, app version), and records of ads viewed and tapped.
  • This information is sent directly to Google and is not passed to the developer. The developer can only see statistics such as total ad revenue.
  • In regions that require consent, such as the European Economic Area and the UK, a Google consent form asks about personalized ads on first launch, and you can change your choice at any time under More > Ad privacy settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID under your device's Settings > Google > Ads to stop receiving personalized ads.
  • The app is set to receive only ads suitable for all ages.
  • How Google uses information from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

3-1. In-app purchases (Android app)

Premium, Remove ads and Make an offering under More are paid through Google Play.

  • Payment information such as your card number, name and email is handled by Google Play and is not passed to the developer. It is governed by Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
  • The app receives only the purchase confirmation from Google Play (what was bought and a purchase token), verifies it on the device, and stores only whether Premium or Remove ads was bought and the number of offerings. Nothing is sent to a developer server.
  • Refunds can be requested from Google Play under Google Play's refund policy. Information needed for refunds is also handled by Google Play.
  • The developer can see sales statistics (counts and amounts) in the Play Console.
  • Purchases are optional. Reading, chanting and practice features all work without paying.
  • Premium's tracing save turns your finished tracing sheet into an image and saves it only to your device's photo folder (Pictures/경전함); it is not sent anywhere. Printing uses your device's print feature (a printer or Save as PDF).

4. YouTube chanting videos

For some sutras, tapping Video shows chanting videos posted to YouTube by official channels such as BBS and BTN in the YouTube player.

  • The app connects to YouTube (Google) only when you tap this button, and makes no connection before that.
  • When it connects, YouTube may receive your IP address, browser information and so on. The app uses YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), but YouTube may use cookies once a video plays.
  • This information is not passed to the developer and is governed by YouTube's Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
  • The videos are copyrighted by their channels; the app does not save videos or extract their audio.

5. Chanting audio

Chanting plays pre-made recordings in AI voices (ElevenLabs). In the Android app the audio is built into the app, so listening makes no outside connection. The web version downloads the audio from the web hosting described below; it does not connect to or send information to ElevenLabs.

If you choose to download the English chanting or Premium's female voice in the Android app, that audio is downloaded to your device from the web hosting below (GitHub Pages). No personal information is sent, and the downloaded audio is stored only on your device.

5-1. More apps you may like

To show "More apps you may like" under More (other apps by the same developer), the app downloads an app list file and app icons from the web hosting below (GitHub Pages) once a day. No personal information is sent.

  • Whether a sibling app is installed is checked only on the device (so it can be opened directly); the result is not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Tapping an item opens that app, the Google Play Store or the app's web page, which are then governed by their own privacy policies.

6. Web hosting

The web version is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When web pages and audio are downloaded, GitHub may keep access logs (such as IP addresses) for operating and securing its service; this information is not passed to the developer. It is governed by the GitHub General Privacy Statement (https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

6-1. Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK and Switzerland

  • As described above, the developer does not collect or store personal information. Ad-related information is processed by Google, which acts as an independent controller for it.
  • The legal basis for personalized ads is your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). On first launch you can accept or refuse in Google's consent form, and the app works fully either way (if you refuse, you may see non-personalized or fewer ads).
  • Withdrawing consent: you can change or withdraw your choice at any time under More > Ad privacy settings. Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before it.
  • Your rights: you may request access to, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection to processing and portability of your data. For ad information processed by Google, use your Google Account settings or the methods in Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy); for anything else, write to the email below. Because the developer receives no data from your device, information stored on the device is deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • Complaints: you may lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of the country where you live.
  • International transfers: information processed by Google may be transferred outside the EEA (for example to the United States); Google applies safeguards required by law, such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. Permissions

  • Internet: to load YouTube chanting videos and banner ads, and to download English chanting or female-voice audio and the "More apps" list (once a day). Reading sutras, listening to chanting, search and practice records work without the internet.
  • Advertising ID: for the banner ads above (Google AdMob).
  • Foreground service (media playback) and wake lock: to keep chanting playing when the screen is off. A notification is shown only while it plays.
  • Notifications: to send Today's verse, Buddhist holy days and 49-day rite days as notifications. Requested only when you turn them on, and you can turn them off at any time.
  • Vibration: to signal each bead or bow and each completed round. It can be turned off on the practice screen.
  • Billing (Google Play Billing): to pay for Premium, Remove ads and offerings through Google Play.
  • Alarms and reminders, run at startup: to sound Premium's chanting alarm at the set time and to set it again after the phone restarts. Active only if you add an alarm.
  • Motion sensors (no permission): used only while counting, to count bows by the phone's tilt in Premium's automatic 108-bow counter. Sensor values are not stored or sent; only the count stays on the device.
